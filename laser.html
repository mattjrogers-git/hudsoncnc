What we do

Cutting, engraving and marking

The laser pairs with our CNC router. The router handles thick material and big sheets, and the laser takes the fine work, so you get both from one shop.

Laser engraving

Logos, text, photos and patterns burned into wood, acrylic, leather, paper and coated surfaces. Good for signs, tags, awards, gifts and branded products.

Laser cutting

Clean, tight-radius cuts in plywood, Baltic birch and acrylic up to ¼″ thick. Small parts, interlocking kits, inlays, templates and architectural models.

Marking & short runs

Repeatable engraving on parts you supply, from a few pieces to a few thousand. We keep your file on hand so reorders match.

Cut + engrave together

Engraved detail and a cut outline in one setup, so a tag, coaster or sign comes off the bed finished.

Laser + CNC

Engrave fine lettering with the laser, cut the thick backer on the router, and assemble in-house. One quote, one shop.

Design help

Send a logo, sketch or photo. We’ll vectorize it, size it for the laser and show you the layout before we run it.

Materials

What the laser works with

Cut and engrave

Baltic birch and other plywood, hardwoods and veneers, acrylic, MDF, leather, paper, cardboard and fabric, up to ¼″ for cutting.

Engrave only

Glass, stone, tile, slate and ceramic, anodized aluminum and powder-coated or painted metal. We can mark them but not cut them.

Needs the router

Anything thicker than ¼″, bare metal, HDPE, foam and composites. We cut those on the CNC router instead.

Not laser-safe

PVC and vinyl release toxic fumes, so we don’t laser them. Not sure about a material? Ask before you buy it.
