Ways contractors use us

Jobs you can send out

Panel cutting & drilling

Cut-to-size sheet goods, nested parts, hole patterns and hardware boring, dados and rabbets. Pressure rollers hold warped construction plywood flat. We don’t build full cabinets, but we’ll cut and drill any panel you need.

Carved architectural details

Dentil molding, corbels, brackets, rosettes, cornice and frieze details, and restoration pieces matched from a sample or photo.

Decorative roof & exterior trim

Gable trim and vergeboards, scroll brackets, gable vents and louvers, rafter tails, finials and cupola parts, in wood, PVC trim board or HDU.

Curved forms & templates

Curved concrete and landscape forms, stair stringers, rafter and truss templates, routing templates and bending forms.

Facade & exterior panels

Fiber cement (HardieBoard), ACM and HPL panels cut and drilled for rainscreen and facade systems.

Commercial interiors

Fluted and 3D wall panels, reception desk parts, store fixtures and slat walls. We’ve fabricated for retail store projects with Buxus Construction.

Signage

Job-site signs and permanent signs for finished projects.

Why send out your CNC work

Less site time, less waste

  • Repeatable accuracy on every part
  • Faster installs and fewer site cuts
  • Nested layouts that use less material
  • One-offs or full production runs
  • Local pickup or Hudson Valley delivery
