Services

What we make

CNC routing, drilling, carving and engraving for builders, designers, artists and businesses. Every job runs on our 5′ × 10′ automatic tool-change router.

Architectural & commercialProducts & production runsSigns, trails & community3D carving & parametric designSlab flattening & wood processingDesign & CAD support

Architectural & commercial

Feature walls, carved and fluted panels, screens, reception desks, store fixtures and millwork for architects, designers and general contractors.

  • Carved, fluted, slotted and acoustic wall panels
  • Decorative screens and room dividers
  • Store fixtures, slat walls and displays
  • Millwork parts, furniture and casework components (we cut the parts; we don’t build full cabinets)

Products & production runs

Repeatable parts for makers and manufacturers. We nest parts tightly to save material and keep programs on file for reorders.

  • Point-of-purchase displays and sales demo kits (for brands)
  • Product parts and flat-pack kits
  • Prototypes, trade-show pieces and short runs
  • Batch and repeat production
  • Jigs, fixtures and templates

Signs, trails & community

Carved and routed signs in wood, HDU and two-color HDPE, from single trail markers to full storefront systems.

  • Trail markers and park signs
  • Business and storefront signs
  • Dimensional letters and logos
  • Plaques, donor walls and memorials

3D carving & parametric design

3D relief carving from models or scans, rotary 4th-axis work, and parametric pieces built from CNC-cut profiles.

  • 3D relief and sculptural carving
  • Parametric wall art and furniture
  • Terrain and topographic models
  • Rotary carving for posts, columns and turned parts

Slab flattening & wood processing

Big live-edge slabs flattened on the router and cut to a traced outline, for tables, counters and bar tops.

  • Slab flattening and surfacing
  • Profile cutting from a traced outline
  • Edge profiles and joinery
  • Inlays and resin pockets

Design & CAD support

Send a napkin sketch or a finished shop drawing. We’ll draw it, fix it for CNC, nest it and tell you what it costs before we cut.

  • Vectorizing sketches, photos and logos
  • Converting shop drawings for CNC
  • Nesting to cut material cost
  • 3D modeling for carving
Capabilities

The toolbox

  • CNC routing and 2D profiling
  • Drilling, boring and hardware holes
  • Nesting and cutting full sheets
  • Pocketing, dados and rabbets
  • V-carving and engraving
  • 3D relief carving
  • Rotary 4th-axis carving
  • Inlays and dimensional letters
  • Slab flattening
  • Prototypes to production runs
Materials

What we cut

Wood & plywood

Hardwoods (oak, walnut, maple, cherry), Baltic birch, cabinet-grade and construction plywood, MDF (standard, moisture-resistant, formaldehyde-free, fire-rated), bamboo plywood.

Plastics & acrylics

Acrylic, polycarbonate, HDPE and two-color sign HDPE, LDPE, marine board (Polywood), nylon, PVC board.

Architectural

HPL laminate, phenolic, solid surface, ACM (aluminum composite), aluminum sheet, fiber cement (HardieBoard).

Sign & carving

HDU and sign foam for carved and dimensional signs, plus carbon fiber for specialty parts.
