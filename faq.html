FAQ

Common questions

What files do you accept?

DXF, DWG, SVG, AI and PDF for 2D work, and STL or OBJ for 3D carving. A sketch, photo or sample to match works too. We can draw it up.

Can you help with design?

Yes. We can turn a sketch into a cut file, adjust your drawings for CNC, or design from scratch.

What’s the largest piece you can cut?

Sheets up to 5′ × 10′ in one piece, and material up to about 8"–12" thick depending on the part.

What materials do you cut?

Wood and plywood, MDF, plastics (acrylic, polycarbonate, HDPE, PVC and more), HPL, phenolic, solid surface, ACM, aluminum, fiber cement, sign foam and HDU.

Do you do one-offs or only production?

Both: single pieces, prototypes and full production runs.

Can I supply my own material?

Yes, or we can source it for you.

How long does a job take?

It depends on the size of the job and our schedule. Tell us your deadline and we’ll give you a date with your quote.

Do you paint, finish or install?

We can prime, paint and clear-coat many pieces, and can help arrange installation. Ask with your quote.

Do you deliver?

Local pickup at our New Paltz shop, or delivery around the Hudson Valley.

Do you build cabinets?

We don’t build full cabinets, but we’ll cut and drill any panels you need.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form or call 845-384-2994. Include drawings or photos, material, quantity and deadline.
